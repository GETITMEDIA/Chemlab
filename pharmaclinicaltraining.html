Overview

Synthiya Research Labs, in collaboration with MOTT Bio Research Lab and Innospecs Bioresearch Private Limited, offers a comprehensive industrial training program designed specifically for college students. This program integrates theoretical knowledge with practical industrial exposure, preparing students for careers in pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and clinical research sectors.

Program Overview

  • The training program focuses on three major domains:
    • Pharmaceutical Analysis
    • Plant Tissue Culture
    • Clinical Research & Study Procedures
  • The program is conducted jointly by all three institutions, ensuring a multidisciplinary learning experience with access to advanced infrastructure and expert guidance.

Objectives of the Training

  • To provide hands-on industrial experience
  • To enhance practical laboratory skills
  • To bridge the gap between academics and industry
  • To develop research and analytical abilities
  • To prepare students for career opportunities in life sciences

Conclusion

This comprehensive industrial training program is designed to equip students with practical knowledge, technical expertise, and industry exposure. Through the combined efforts of all three institutions, participants gain valuable insights and skills necessary to succeed in the rapidly evolving fields of pharmaceutical analysis, biotechnology, and clinical research.
