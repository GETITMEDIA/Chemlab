// QUESTION 1 OF 5 — HEMATOLOGY
A patient's CBC shows MCV of 110 fL and hypersegmented neutrophils on peripheral smear. Which deficiency is MOST likely responsible?
02:00
A
Iron deficiency
B
Vitamin B12 or Folate deficiency
C
Vitamin D deficiency
D
Copper deficiency
Correct Answer: B — Vitamin B12 or Folate deficiency

An MCV above 100 fL indicates macrocytosis. Combined with hypersegmented neutrophils (≥5 lobes in >3% of cells or any neutrophil with ≥6 lobes), this is the classic picture of megaloblastic anemia caused by B12 or Folate deficiency. These vitamins are essential for DNA synthesis; their absence impairs nuclear maturation while cytoplasmic development continues normally.
