Our laboratory is approved by CDSCO and AYUSH, ISO 9001:2015 certified, and NABL accredited, ensuring reliable, high-quality testing for pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and traditional products in compliance with national and international standards.

To provide a comfortable and safe environment for our patients, please avoid wearing scented perfumes or creams when visiting us.

CDSCO (FORM 37)

CDSCO (FORM 37) approval for testing Pharmaceuticals. Ensures compliance with national regulatory standards for safe and reliable product testing.

AYUSH (FORM 48)

AYUSH (FORM 48) approval for testing AYUSH products. Authorizes quality evaluation of traditional medicine systems like Ayurveda and Siddha.

ISO 9001:2015 ( Quality Management System)

ISO 9001:2015 certification for Quality Management System. Demonstrates commitment to consistent quality processes and continuous improvement.

NABL Accreditation (NABL 120)

NABL Accreditation (NABL 120) for Drugs, Cosmetics, and Essential Oils.
Confirms technical competence and adherence to international laboratory standards.

COS 23 Licence approved by CDSCO for Cosmetic product testing. Permits certified testing of cosmetic products ensuring safety and compliance.
