AYUSH Product Analysis

The quality of traditional medicines plays a critical role in consumer confidence and regulatory acceptance.
Synthiya Research Labs provides analytical testing services for Ayurveda, Siddha, and Unani products covering raw materials, in-process materials, and finished formulations.
Testing includes physicochemical evaluation, identification testing, assay determination, microbial analysis, heavy metal screening, and stability assessment.
Our laboratory supports AYUSH manufacturers in maintaining product quality and compliance with applicable requirements.
