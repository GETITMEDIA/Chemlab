Company Overview

Established in 2012, Synthiya Research Labs Private Limited was founded with a vision to bridge the gap between scientific testing excellence and industry requirements. Over the years, the laboratory has evolved from a pharmaceutical testing facility into a multidisciplinary analytical center serving pharmaceutical, cosmetic, AYUSH, essential oil, and research sectors. Through continuous investment in infrastructure, scientific expertise, and quality systems, Synthiya Research Labs has built a reputation for reliability, technical competence, and customer-focused service.
