Analytical Method Development & Validation Project

New Advanced Instruments

This training program by Synthiya Research Labs Private Limited focuses on developing and validating analytical methods used in pharmaceutical analysis, particularly using advanced techniques like RP-HPLC and UHPLC. Participants will learn how to select suitable analytical methods based on product characteristics, optimize conditions such as wavelength, mobile phase, and column type, and perform validation studies following International Council for Harmonisation guidelines. The program also includes hands-on work in evaluating parameters like accuracy, precision, linearity, robustness, and detection limits.

In addition to practical experimentation, the training emphasizes proper documentation and reporting, which are essential in regulatory environments. Students will compile calculations, chromatograms, and validation data into a structured Method Validation Report that meets industry standards. Overall, this program equips learners with both technical skills and compliance knowledge needed for careers in pharmaceutical quality control and analytical research.

Analytical Method Workflow

1. Method Selection
Choosing RP-HPLC / UHPLC
Selection depends on analyte polarity, solubility, and required sensitivity.
2. Method Optimization
λ-max Determination
Wavelength where compound shows maximum absorbance.
Mobile Phase Optimization
Adjust solvent ratio to achieve proper separation.
Column Selection (C18 / C8)
Stationary phase selection based on compound nature.
Flow Rate & Injection Volume
Optimized to improve resolution and peak shape.
3. Method Validation (ICH)
Assay
Quantitative determination of active compound.
System Suitability
Ensures system performance before analysis.
Accuracy
Closeness to true value.
Precision
Repeatability and reproducibility.
Linearity & Range
Response proportional to concentration.
Specificity
No interference from impurities.
Robustness
Method stability under small changes.
LOD & LOQ
Detection and quantification limits.
Solution Stability
Stability of prepared solution over time.
Stress Study
Forced degradation under stress conditions.
4. Documentation
Reports & Chromatograms
Complete validation report with calculations, graphs, and chromatograms.
