Cosmetic Product Evaluation

Cosmetic products must meet stringent quality and safety requirements before reaching consumers.
Our laboratory provides testing services for creams, lotions, shampoos, soaps, hair oils, baby care products, lip care products, and other cosmetic formulations.
Testing parameters include pH, viscosity, moisture content, total fatty matter, microbial quality, heavy metal analysis, and stability studies.
Our services help manufacturers demonstrate product quality, safety, and consistency throughout the product lifecycle.

Categories of Cosmetic Products for Testing

The cosmetic products approved under the COS-23 licence are classified into the following categories:

S.No Form/Type Area of Use Main Purpose Examples
a Dry (loose/pressed) Face, body Skin finishing, oil control Face powder, body powder
b Dry, very fine Baby skin Protection, prevent rashes Baby powder
c Semi-solid / Liquid Skin, hair Moisturizing, cleansing, conditioning Creams, lotions, shampoos
d Liquid Nails Beautification, coloring Nail polish, lacquer
e Semi-solid Lips Coloring, protection, shine Lipstick, lip gloss
f Cream / Lotion Skin (body hair areas) Hair removal Hair removal cream
g Liquid / Semi-solid Eyes Eye makeup, enhancement Kajal, eyeliner, mascara
h Spray form Skin / Hair Easy application (spray delivery) Spray deodorant, hair spray

Conclusion

The COS-23 licence ensures that all these categories of cosmetic products undergo proper testing for safety, quality, and compliance. This classification helps regulatory authorities maintain standards and protect consumer health.
