Laboratory Orientation and Safety Training Program

New Advanced Instruments

Students are introduced to the laboratory environment, where the purpose of the training program and its overall schedule are explained. Basic rules and expectations inside the lab are clearly communicated to all trainees. Students are informed that safety is the highest priority, and everyone must strictly follow the instructions given by the trainer. Eating, drinking, and unnecessary mobile phone usage are strictly prohibited to maintain discipline and safety.

Wearing a lab coat is mandatory during all lab activities, and proper dress code must be followed at all times. Gloves and goggles must be used whenever handling chemicals. Instruments should not be operated without the trainer’s permission. Students are also taught the basics of Good Laboratory Practices (GLP). Participants who successfully complete the training program will be awarded an official Certificate of Completion.
