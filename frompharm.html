PRACTICAL TRAINING MODULES

New Advanced Instruments

Pharmaceutical analysis plays a critical role in ensuring that drug substances and formulations are safe, effective, and meet quality standards. However, several analytical challenges arise during the evaluation of pharmaceutical products.

One of the primary difficulties is the accurate quantification of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) such as Paracetamol and Ibuprofen in complex formulations. Excipients and formulation matrices can interfere with measurements, making precise determination challenging.

Another challenge involves the selection of the appropriate analytical wavelength (λ-max) in spectrophotometric methods. Incorrect wavelength selection can lead to poor sensitivity, reduced accuracy, and unreliable results.
